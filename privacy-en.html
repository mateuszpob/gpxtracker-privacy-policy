Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Overview

GPX Tracks is designed to process your routes and saved places locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server that stores your GPX files, tracks or waypoints.

No analytics

Firebase Analytics has been removed from GPX Tracks. The app does not use analytics services and does not collect or send app usage statistics.

GPX files, tracks and waypoints

Recorded tracks, opened or imported GPX files, and saved waypoints — including titles, descriptions, icons and optional links to a track — are processed and stored locally on your device.

Opening, importing, editing and exporting are local app operations. GPX Tracks does not upload this content to the developer. If you share an exported file through Android or another app, the receiving app handles that copy according to its own policy.

Location data

The app uses ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION to display your position and record tracks. Recording started while the app is visible can continue through a foreground service after the screen is minimized. Recorded location points remain in the local database on your device.

GPX Tracks does not send recorded coordinates, waypoint coordinates or route history to the developer. Online map providers may receive requests for the map area you view and normal network metadata. Those requests are governed by the providers’ own policies.

Elevation data

Track elevation statistics use altitude recorded by your device’s GPS. Calculations are performed locally.

Map data and external services

Online maps use OpenStreetMap data and the configured map tile provider. Viewing online maps may send the requested map area and standard network metadata to that provider.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Permissions

  • Location: displaying your position and recording tracks.
  • Files: opening, reading and exporting GPX files.

Storage and deletion

Routes, location points, statistics, folders and waypoints are stored locally on your device. You can delete local tracks using the app controls. Deleted waypoints disappear from the map and list, but their records remain in the local database. You can remove app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app; Android or your chosen backup provider may retain a copy according to your device and account settings.

Because GPX Tracks does not send route or waypoint content to the developer, there is no developer-side copy of that content to delete. Exported or shared files are handled by the destination you choose.

Children

The app is intended for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the relevant feature is released. Questions can be sent to trackergpx@gmail.com.
