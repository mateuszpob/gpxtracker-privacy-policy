Frequently asked questions

A short guide to recording tracks, using waypoints and working with GPX files.

Get it on Google PlayHome

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without an account and stores tracks and waypoints locally.

Does the app send my coordinates?

No. Saved tracks, GPS points and waypoints stay on your phone and are not sent to the developer. When you use an online map, its provider receives a request for the map area you view and standard connection data.

What does Firebase Analytics do?

It provides optional usage statistics. You can turn it off in settings. It does not include tracks, coordinates or waypoint content.

Does the app provide turn-by-turn navigation?

GPX Tracks records and displays tracks and points. It is not a step-by-step navigation app.

How do I open a GPX file?

Select a GPX file in your file manager and open it with GPX Tracks. Previewing a file and importing it are separate actions.

How do I delete a waypoint?

Delete it from the waypoint list. The record remains marked as deleted to protect application data.

Adding a waypoint

  1. Long press the chosen place on the map.
  2. Enter a title and optional description.
  3. Choose an icon and save the point.
  4. Open the waypoint list to find or edit it.
